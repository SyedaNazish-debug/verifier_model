**PROJECT ABSTRACT**

**Problem:**

In the era of digital reforms and AI-driven technologies, the wide spread of misinformation and fake resources has been increased by the boost of social platforms. 
This brings us to the most challenging phase to prevent this misleading core from distracting the whole generation. 

**Purpose:** 

This project “VERIFIER LENSE MODEL” aims to leverage the modern machine learning technologies & natural language processing to classify, predict & flag the resources such as
news articles, journals, viral posts as fake or real based on its language & conceptual context through textual data that uses the basic feature engineering concepts to harness precision in labelled datasets for training.  

**Methodologies/Approaches:**

The methodologies involved here are from the basic python programming & it's useful libraries, necessary dataset from Kaggle, feature engineering for data extraction, different API’S for real analysis & verification. 

**Conclusion/outcomes:** 

The system has the potential to leverage deployment in real time verification that contributed to reduction of misinformation by the higher accuracy rate of 92%. 

In conclusion, this system helps fake and misleading information detection effectively to increase more reliable resources.
