ABOUT VERIFIER LENSE MODEL

Making news verification More accessibale and easier for everyone.

Verifier Lense Model is a machine learning based fake news detection system designed to classify news content as real or fake. The systemuses a trainedclassification model to analyze and predict the authenticity of news articles. It uses TF-IDF vectorization for text preprocessing and a machine learning model for prediction. The system is designed to be user-friendly and accessible to everyone, making it easier to verify the authenticity of news articles.
